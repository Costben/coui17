## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `SlideView` (experimental): the ColorOS 17 swipe-to-delete list row. Swiping reveals full-height actions behind the content, with the native drag damping past the menu, release spring and delete animation; `SlideViewState.delete()` slides the row out and collapses it, and `fastDeleteEnabled` deletes on a long slide.
- `LocalBlurEnabled` and `isBlurEnabled()` in `coui-blur` provide a global one-line toggle for backdrop blur (symmetric with `LocalSquircleEnabled` / `isSquircleEnabled()`), combined with Android's system material-blur and power-saving state. `LayerBackdrop` now tracks active blur consumers (including chained `rememberLayerBackdrop(upstream = ...)` layers) and skips `layerBackdrop` recording whenever nothing samples it; disabling blur also reverts `TopAppBar`, `SmallTopAppBar`, and `AppBarBlurOverlay` to opaque container fills instead of translucent unblurred gradients.
- `Colors.containerTheme` (`couiColorContainerTheme`, `#0080FF` / `#1A8CFF`) and the `focus` / `focusOutline` mask and ring the COUI state drawables use (`#1F0080FF` / `#331A8CFF` and `#660080FF` / `#665C9DFF`); the 1.1.0 constructor and `copy` stay as hidden overloads that fall back to `secondaryContainer`.
- `COUIIndication` draws the focus ring `COUIStrokeDrawable` draws — 3dp of `focusOutline` stroked on the control's edge — and takes `focusOutline` and the `shape` the ring follows.
- `SwitchPreference` takes `showRedDot` (the 6dp `jump_icon_red_dot` before the switch) and `onClick` (the preference click listener, also dispatched when the switch itself is toggled).
- `SwitchLoadingPreference` starts its loading spinner on the tap instead of waiting for the caller to flip `isLoading`.
- `IntentSeekBar` takes `playbackState` and reproduces all five `COUIIntentSeekBar.VisualState`s: the plain track, the playing / dragging cone light, and the full and partial loading sweeps (`LoadingEffectParams` 0.6 length, 0.6 mid alpha).
- `scrollEndHaptic` performs the ColorOS 307 `EDGE_LIST_VIBRATE` waveform on Android, falling back to the Compose haptic type on builds that reject the constant.
- `NavigationBarDefaults.blurColors` / `blurRadius` carry the `FRAMEWORK_BOTTOM_BAR_PARAMS_*` recipe the navigation bar blurs with.
- `Chip` takes `count`, the trailing count text COUIChipDrawable draws after the label (12sp `COUNT_TEXT_SIZE`, 2dp `mCountMarginStart`), and `showRedDot`, the 6dp `COUIHintRedDot.Small` dot pinned to the label's top-end corner with `coui_chip_red_dot_offset_horizontal` 2dp / `_vertical` 6dp. The count is spoken as part of the chip (`coui_chip_count_separator`), the close icon now grows in on the `COUIChipDrawable.CloseIconAnimation` spring (response 0.3 / bounce 0, scaled from 0.3) and widens the chip with it. `ChipDefaults` gains `CountMarginStart`, `CountSeparator`, `CountTextSize`, `RedDotSize`, `RedDotOffsetHorizontal`, `RedDotOffsetVertical`, `RedDotReservedWidth`, `countTextStyle()` and `redDotColor()`.
- `TextFieldColors` gains `disabledBorderColor` (`coui_textinput_stroke_color_disabled`, `#19000000` / `#19FFFFFF`) and `labelCutoutColor`, the patch painted behind a floating rectangle-mode label to break the outline (`coui_textinput_label_cutout_padding` 4dp). An error field now also fades in COUI's selection mask (133ms after 80ms, alpha 0 to 0.3 on `COUIEaseInterpolator`). `TextFieldDefaults.RectangleEndInsideMargin` is the 10dp right padding of Widget.COUI.EditText.HintAnim.Rectangle.
- `InputView`'s multi-line card reserves `coui_card_multi_input_height` (`InputViewDefaults.MultiFieldHeight`, 152dp), so a short draft still fills the card.
- The clickable `Card` takes `selected`, the locked selected state of `COUICardListSelectedItemLayout.setIsSelected`: the fill stays at `couiColorCardPressed` instead of returning to rest.
- `FloatingActionButtonDefaults` spells out the COUIFABPressFeedbackUtil contract: `PressedScale` 0.9, `PressDuration` 200ms, `ReleaseDuration` 340ms and the `COUIMoveEase` curve, plus `ShadowColor` (`coui_floating_button_elevation_color`).
- `PageIndicator` reproduces COUIPageIndicator's four-rung size ladder (6dp / 4.5dp / 2.5dp / gone, eased with `COUIEaseInterpolator`), its 1500-stiffness position spring (`minimumVisibleChange` 0.005), the 5% trace-colour cross-fade and an accessibility description. `PageIndicatorDefaults` gains `PositionStiffness`, `MinimumVisibleChange`, `ColorFactorThreshold` and `LadderSlots`; the new `contentDescription` parameter replaces the built-in text.
- `SliderDefaults` gains `MinTouchHeight` (36dp, `couiSeekBarMinHeight`), `PercentRange` (`0f..100f`, COUI's `DEFAULT_MAX`), `FlingVelocityLimit` and `FlingProjectionSeconds`. `Slider` takes `showProgress` / `showThumb` (`couiSeekBarShowProgress` / `couiSeekBarShowThumb`), `startFromMiddle` (`couiSeekBarStartMiddle`) and `moveByFinger` (`MOVE_BY_FINGER`).
- `IconButtonDefaults.IconPadding` (8dp, COUIIconButton `iconPadding`), and `IconButton` takes an `interactionSource`.
- `HighlightedIconButtonDefaults.TopBarBlurSigmaPx`, the 20px sigma of `TYPE_FRAMEWORK_TOP_BAR_BLUR`.
- `Stepper` repeats while a button is held: the click fires at once and then every 100ms after a 500ms hold, as COUIStepperView's LongPressProxy does. The API is unchanged.
- `GridSpringChainDefaults.GridAttachmentTension`, the 150 neighbour tension `COUIGridSpringChain` passes to `COUISpringChain.create`.
- `Badge` announces its number (`COUIHintRedDot.setPointNumber`) and `Switch` announces its loading state (`couiswitch_loading`).

### Fixed

A slider row was showing 40dp between its title and its track — the row's bottom inset, the action's own inset, an 8dp spacer and the track centred in its 36dp touch target, all counted at once — where ColorOS 17 draws 8dp. `SliderPreference` and every other preference that takes a `bottomAction` now keep the inside margin only above the text and below the action.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- `Chip` close/dismiss affordance with its own touch target.
- `Slider` secondary progress tier (`secondaryProgress`), mirrored on the range and vertical
  sliders where the track geometry is shared.
- A `Tiny` size tier for `Button`.
- `error` and `pause` states for `CircularProgressIndicator`.
- A centred warning dialog variant (overlay and window hosts).
- A horizontal progress dialog variant (overlay and window hosts).
- A custom content slot for `TopTips` alongside the existing marquee behaviour.
- `enabled` on `RecommendedPreference`, with a disabled visual state.
- Binary compatibility validation: every published module now ships a committed API dump.
- The new standalone API in this release carries the `@ExperimentalCOUIApi` opt-in marker, so
  callers opt in explicitly and the surface may still change in a minor release. Markers are not
  applied to types that already appear in a stable signature, and Kotlin rejects opt-in markers on
  value parameters, so a parameter added to an existing composable is flagged in its KDoc instead.

### Changed

- Dokka documentation is generated for every published module, the published `-javadoc` artifacts
  now contain the real API documentation instead of an empty archive, and the documentation site
  exposes the API reference of all nine modules.
- Publication is staged into a single Maven Central bundle that is validated locally before upload.

### Fixed

- Restored source and binary compatibility with 1.1.0. Every parameter added in 1.2.0 now carries a
  default, and every public JVM member the 1.1.0 artifacts exposed is declared again, so an
  application compiled against 1.1.0 keeps linking and running without a rebuild. Two
  compiler-generated lambda accessors inside `ComposableSingletons$TopAppBarKt` cannot be
  re-declared; they are referenced only from `TopAppBarKt` inside the library, never from consumer
  bytecode, so no application can link against them.
- The release workflow no longer combines tag triggers with a path filter, which previously
  prevented tagged releases from running at all.
- The Android baseline profiles now resolve against the current library: entries that named
  classes removed or renamed before 1.2.0 are updated or dropped, so the shipped profiles no longer
  spend startup budget on classes that do not exist.
- Removed the redundant clock settling in `NavNestedStressTest`. The helper advanced the manual test
  clock roughly 4x past the driver's own settle curves and settled a second time after every arm was
  already at rest, so its wall time grew with machine load until it crossed the framework's 60s
  test-body timeout. The test now settles exactly as far as the driver's tween and spring need.
- Dropped the now-redundant `-Xcontext-parameters` opt-in from `coui-blur`, which is no longer
  required by the Kotlin version in use.
- Removed an unnecessary safe call in `SearchBar`'s action slot.
- The sample app no longer reaches for the lock-screen wallpaper on Android versions that do not
  provide it, and the wallpaper lookups no longer trip the missing-permission lint error.
